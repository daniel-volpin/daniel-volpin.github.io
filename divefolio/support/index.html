Support

Where is my data stored?

Dive records, photos, certification cards, gear, and your diver profile are stored locally on the device. Divefolio does not currently provide an account or cloud sync.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings → Back Up Divefolio Package and save the .divefolio package somewhere you control, such as iCloud Drive. It contains every dive, photo, certification card, and piece of gear, and your diver profile, including any medical notes and emergency contacts, so keep it private.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings → Restore .divefolio Backup and choose a .divefolio package, or a .json archive from before photos existed. Divefolio shows what the backup adds and what it replaces before anything changes, and asks you to confirm. Nothing is deleted.

You can also open a .divefolio backup or a .uddf logbook straight from Files, Mail, or AirDrop: Divefolio opens Settings and restores or imports it the same way.

Where are my C-cards and gear?

In the Diver tab. It holds your diver profile and C-cards, your gear bag and its service dates, and the scuba calculator.

How do I switch between metres and feet?

Open Settings → Units and choose metric or imperial. Dives are always stored in metres and Celsius, so changing units never changes a dive.

Why is a dive named after a nearby place?

When Apple Watch brings in a dive with an entry point, Apple Maps suggests a name for the site, such as the lake or reef nearby. Rename it like any other dive.

A dive from Apple Watch is missing

Check Health → Sharing → Apps → Divefolio and allow depth and workout data, then open Divefolio again.

A dive that reached your iPhone days late, such as from a trip without it, comes in after you tap Stop Syncing, then Sync Dives from Apple Watch, in Divefolio's Settings.

Can Divefolio replace my dive computer?

No. Divefolio does not calculate decompression limits, no-fly intervals, residual nitrogen, or safe-to-dive recommendations. Its gas calculators give reference values from standard training formulas, not dive plans.
