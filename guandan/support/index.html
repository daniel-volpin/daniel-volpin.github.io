Support

Guandan Classic is a single-player version of Guandan (掼蛋), the Chinese partnership card game: you and a computer partner against two computer opponents, on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email volpinworks@gmail.com. Please include your device, its iOS version, and the app's version, which About in the app shows.

Questions

How do I win?

Empty your hand before the other team. The team of the first player out climbs one to three levels, and each hand is played at the level of the team that won the last one. Climb from 2 to A, then win a hand played at your own A to take the match; for a shorter match, set new matches to start at 6 or 10 under New matches in Settings. How to Play, in the app, has the full rules.

How do I play cards?

Tap cards to pick a play, slide a finger across cards to pick several, or hold one to take its whole column, then tap Play. Tap Pass to let a trick go by; you can't pass when you lead. Hint suggests a play, and tapping it again suggests the next one. When you hold a bomb that needs no wildcard, the flame button above your hand sets each bomb apart in a column of its own and leaves your wildcards free; tap it again to put them back.

What is tribute?

Before each hand after the first, the last player out gives the first player out their strongest card and gets back a card ranked 10 or lower. After a team takes 1st and 2nd, both of the other team's players pay. When you receive tribute, the app asks which card to give back. You can turn tribute off in Settings.

What are the stricter rules at A?

An option in Settings, off unless you turn it on. With it, a win at your own A takes the match only when neither you nor your partner finishes last, and a team that misses at its own A three times goes back to 2.

Can I change the difficulty?

Yes, under Computer players in Settings: Easy, Normal, or Hard. On Normal and Hard, your partner and both opponents play at that level. On Easy, only the opponents make mistakes; your partner plays at Normal.

Can I see which cards are still out?

Yes. During a hand, tap the button beside the menu at the top right of the table. Cards Left counts how many cards of each rank the other players still hold, with the wildcards apart, and History lists every play of the hand, trick by trick. The computer players wait while it is open.

Where are my statistics?

Statistics, on the main menu, shows the matches and hands you have won at each difficulty, with quick matches counted apart from full ones, how often you finish in each place, and highlights such as your longest run of hands won. They stay on your device, and Reset Statistics starts them over.

Can I look back at a hand after it ends?

Yes. The result of each hand shows the cards everyone still held, and Review the Hand lists every play of the hand, trick by trick.

Can the computer players play faster or slower?

Yes, under Computer players in Settings: Slow, Medium, or Fast. Under Table, Pass automatically passes for you after a moment when nothing in your hand beats the play.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

The app follows your device's language. To choose English or 简体中文 for this app alone, open the app's page in the Settings app and pick a language under Language. Language in the app's own Settings takes you there. If the page has no Language option, first add a second language in General › Language & Region.

Can I continue a match later?

Yes. Your match is saved after every move; tap Continue on the main menu.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Guandan Classic is fully offline and single-player, with no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
