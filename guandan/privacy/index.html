Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Guandan Classic collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app keeps three things on your iPhone or iPad, only so that it works:

  • Your settings: the computer players' difficulty and speed, the level new matches start at, tribute, the stricter rules at A, passing automatically, whether your bombs are set apart, haptics, sound, and whether you have seen the welcome screen.
  • Your match in progress, with the plays of the hand on the table, so that you can continue it. It is deleted when the match ends.
  • Your statistics: the hands and matches you finished, your places, and highlights such as the bombs you played. Reset Statistics, in the app, deletes them.

We never receive any of this. Like other app data, it can be part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

The app shares nothing with anyone. It contains no third-party code, such as analytics or advertising, that could collect or share data.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome at volpinworks@gmail.com.
