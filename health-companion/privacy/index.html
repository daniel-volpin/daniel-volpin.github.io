Health Companion

For iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Health Companion is a local-first nutrition and training assistant.

Health and training data

With your permission, the app reads nutrition, workout, and body measurement data from Apple Health. The app may write food entries that you explicitly confirm. HealthKit remains the source of truth for that data.

The app searches a bundled, read-only NEVO food database on your device. The Gemma model runs on your device to process chat requests.

Optional Hevy sync

If you enable Hevy deep sync and enter a Hevy API key, the app sends requests to Hevy to retrieve your workout data. Detailed Hevy workout-set data is stored in your private iCloud/CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID. The API key is stored in the device Keychain.

Sharing and tracking

Health Companion does not sell health data or use it for advertising tracking. The app does not send HealthKit data to a Health Companion server.

Your choices

You can change HealthKit permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings. You can remove the Hevy API key in the app's Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact volpinworks@gmail.com.
