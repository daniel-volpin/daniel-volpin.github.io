Nutrition and training for iPhone

A conversation, not a wall of forms.

Health Companion is a local-first nutrition and training assistant. Apple Health stays the source of truth, food comes from a bundled nutrition database, and chat runs on your device.

Ask, and log food, in Chat

Ask about nutrition, body measurements, and training time, and get answers from your own Apple Health data. Photo, Describe, Barcode, and Search sit above the message field.

Every food match comes from the bundled, read-only NEVO food database. Choose a match, enter the grams, review the scaled nutrition, and tap Log to Health. Nothing is written to Health before you do, and a likely duplicate asks whether to Keep existing or Log another serving.

A barcode reads a product code only: NEVO has no barcode catalogue, so you pick the matching food by name. Search and logging work without the on-device Gemma model, which you load in Settings for conversation and photo analysis.

Your trends, in Health

The Health tab opens with a plain verdict on whether your plan appears to be working, then shows weight, calories, macros, activity, sleep, and hydration over the range you choose. When there isn't enough history for a trend, it says so rather than inventing one.

Tap What should I change next week? to take the question to Chat.

Optional Hevy sync

Connect Hevy with your API key to sync your workout history, then ask about recent sessions and exercise trends.

Private by design

Health Companion does not sell health data or use it for advertising tracking, and does not send HealthKit data to a Health Companion server. Read the privacy policy.
